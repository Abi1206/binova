The university

The flagship institution for higher education and research in Binod.

BINOVA is the flagship higher-education and research institution of Binod World. It exists so that people can discover what they are capable of becoming, and so that Binod World can develop the knowledge its future will need.

BINOVA exists to teach people to see carefully, grasp why things work, test what they are told, and move knowledge forward. It aims to grow into an institution covering practically every field of study.

Learners advance when they can show what they understand, supported by mentors who know them. There is no class rank and there are no league tables.

Read the philosophy →

Founding principles

What BINOVA is built on.

  1. Education must not merely teach what is already known. It must create the ability to discover what is not yet known.
  2. Every person has a different potential. No one should be forced into a single definition of success.
  3. Practical ability and theoretical knowledge belong together.
  4. Learners advance when they can show what they understand, not when a calendar says so.
  5. Question everything, including this institution.

Featured story Proposed

Learning that begins with looking.

Every programme at BINOVA is proposed to start from the same four practices: look closely before explaining, explain in your own words, test claims including your own, and add something real to the world.

This is a description of the proposed approach, not a research result.
